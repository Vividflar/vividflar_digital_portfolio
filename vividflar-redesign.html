Texture is
the story.

Vividflar is the illustration practice of Paul Nhlanhla Sikhakhane — pop-culture-inspired character work built on close attention to texture, light, and detail. Still learning in public, one piece at a time.

About
the work

South Africa is a country of many flavours, beliefs, and backgrounds, mixing into something vivid — and that's the well I draw from. My subject matter is usually pop-culture and the world directly around me, but the constant underneath any subject is the same: rendering texture and paying attention to detail. That's where the name comes from.

I like experimenting across styles rather than settling into one signature look. I'm still learning and still growing — I see that as a strength, not a caveat, because it means I stay willing to change and take on things I haven't done before.
